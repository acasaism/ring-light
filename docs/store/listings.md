## English

**Name:** Ring Light for Google Meet

**Summary:**

```
Turns your screen into a ring light for Google Meet calls: a soft, adjustable frame of light that lights up your face.
```

**Description:**

```
No lamp in front of you? Ring Light turns your screen into one during Google Meet calls.

When you join a meeting, Meet shrinks slightly and a soft frame of light fills the edges of your screen, lighting up your face. No Meet control is covered, and the light only appears inside a meeting.

Features
• Temperature from cool to warm, and intensity from dim to bright
• Adjustable frame thickness, or no frame at all
• Light up Meet's dark background for even more light
• Light window: a movable window filled with light for a second screen (double-click for full screen)
• Save your favourite settings and go back to them anytime, with undo
• Keyboard shortcut: Alt+Shift+L
• Light and dark themes, accessible panel
• 12 languages

Privacy: no data collected, no network requests. Settings stay in your browser.

Open source (MIT): https://github.com/acasaism/ring-light
```
